Web Publishing: How to Login to the WCM
Overview
Once you have completed the Web Publishers Canvas training course and you are provisioned in, there are two ways to
start editing a webpage in the WCM: login via the Green Dot Shortcut or login via CascadeCMS.com. This article lists
instructions for both.
How to Login via the Green Dot Shortcut
If you know exactly what page you wish to edit on the site, simply navigate to that page and scroll to the very bottom.
There is a small green dot on the left side of the footer. Clicking this dot and logging in with your Sac State username and
password will take you directly to the page's location inside the WCM, allowing you to edit it without searching through
the drop down menu.
How to Login via CascadeCMS.com
Open a browser and navigate to https://csus.cascadecms.com/. Feel free to bookmark the link now for easier access
later.
Login with your Sac State username and password.
Navigate to Site: Go To Site in the upper left-hand corner
