Web Publishing: Using the Connect Bar Functionality in the WCM
Overview
The connect bar is an area towards the bottom of the T4 page that allows anyone visiting the page to know where to find
that office on campus, the hours the office keeps, the phone number, and an option to look at a roster of employees and
their position in the “Meet Us” section.
Instructions
To add to or edit the contact bar:
Navigate to your T4 page in the WCM
Click the “Edit” pencil at the top-right of the page, and then scroll down until you reach “Connect Area.”
Select the arrow to the left of “Connect Area,” and make sure the “Show/Hide” menu is set to “Show.”
Make sure there is something written in the “Header for Connect Area.” This will not display anywhere on the page but
is necessary for accessibility purposes.
There are four areas that make up the connect bar: Find Us, Contacts Us, Meet Us, and Follow Us. “Meet Us” will be
covered in another KB.
Make sure the sections in Find Us are filled out are much as possible. There are a few formatting notes in this section
that need to be followed: under “Building Name Location of Department Office,” write out the full name of the building,
and follow it with a comma and the room number. Do not fill out the room number in the next line down. Any lines left
blank will not show on the page.
Under “Contact Us,” put the phone number for the front desk phone or administrative support assistant if available.
